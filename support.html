Golf Tempo support

Golf Tempo is a practice tool for golf swing tempo. It plays tempo beats on your Apple Watch, measures each swing on your lead wrist, and shows how your swing landed against the beats.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 26 or later and an Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later, paired. Best on Apple Watch Series 8, Ultra, or later, which measure motion at the highest rate.
  • The watch snug on your lead wrist (the left wrist for a right-handed golfer).

Getting started

  1. Open Golf Tempo on the watch. Check handedness and wrist in Settings.
  2. Pick a family (Driver, Long Game, Short Game) and a preset, then tap Start. Allow Health and Motion access when asked.
  3. Swing with the beats. A result shows after each swing. Tap End when you finish.

Common questions

  • Why does it start a workout? The workout keeps the watch measuring and the beats playing when you lower your wrist. Each session is saved to Health as a golf workout.
  • Does it read my Health data? No. It only saves your practice sessions as golf workouts.
  • Can I use it on the course? Practice only. The Rules of Golf do not allow tempo audio during a round (Rule 4.3).
  • Where is my data? On your iPhone and watch only. See the privacy policy.
