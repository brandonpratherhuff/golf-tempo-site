Golf Tempo privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-11. Golf Tempo is made by Brandon Prather-Huff ("we").

The short version

Golf Tempo collects no data. Everything the app measures stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party software in the app.

What the app uses on your devices

  • Wrist motion. The watch reads its motion sensors during a practice session to time each swing. The app processes this on your devices and stores your swing results on your watch and iPhone.
  • Apple Health. With your permission, the watch saves each practice session to Health as a golf workout. It saves the workout only; it does not ask to save heart rate, energy, or other samples. Golf Tempo does not read your Health data. The iPhone app uses Health only to start the workout on your watch. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing and is never sold or shared by us.
  • Between your own devices. Your watch and iPhone exchange settings and swing results directly with each other through Apple's watch connection.
  • Raw motion recordings (optional, testers). Golf Tempo includes a recorder for testers that is off by default (watch Settings > Tester, a section shown after tapping the version line seven times). When you turn on "Record raw motion", each later practice session's wrist motion, beats, results, and a session log (battery level, memory use, and when the session started and ended) are written to a file on your watch and sent to your iPhone over the watch connection. A file holds times counted from the start of the session, with no name or location. The watch checks for free space before writing; that check stays on the watch. On the iPhone the files are kept out of device backups, and you can share or delete them in Settings > Data > Captures.
  • Session export (optional, testers). Once a recording is stored, iPhone Settings > Data also offers "Export sessions": one zip of your practice session files (each swing's result with its date and time), handed to the share sheet when you ask for it.
  • Nothing leaves without you. The app itself sends no recording or export anywhere. A file leaves your iPhone only when you choose a destination in the share sheet (for example Mail, AirDrop, or Files).

The files stay on your devices. The app never sends them anywhere. You can export one yourself with the share sheet; where it goes is your choice, and we do not ask for them.

What Apple may share with us

If you agree to share crash data and app analytics with developers in your device settings, or send feedback through TestFlight, Apple gives us crash reports and your feedback. Apple's own privacy policy governs that data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your devices until you delete it. Turning off "Record raw motion" stops recording from the next session; recordings already made stay until you delete them in iPhone Settings > Data > Captures. Deleting the app deletes its data on that device. Workouts saved to Health stay in Health until you delete them there. You can withdraw Health access in the Health app (Sharing > Apps) and motion access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness.

Children

Golf Tempo is not directed to children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy here with a new effective date.
